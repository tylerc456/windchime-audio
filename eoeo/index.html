How it works

Tap an empty ring to record a take into it — pick a demo sound in this menu first if you want a kick, snare or tap on that ring. Drag a waveform to roll it around its ring and line its start up with the top — it snaps to the red bar when you get close, so quantizing is just “roll until it clicks.” Drag it onto another ring and let go — you’ll get the big Tile / Move / Copy choice. The red bar at the top is where every ring’s section starts and ends, and each waveform’s beginning — the start of the slice, not its loudest point — is drawn as a red segment, so you align beginnings, not peaks. The dot on each ring is its playhead: the centre ring laps in 1 beat, the next in 2, then 4, 8… The round Split button under the stage chops the selected ring’s take into 1, 2, 4, 8 or 16 independently rolling slices.

This is a new song. The loop in focus is the wide donut, split into sections — 4 to start; change it any time with the round Split button under the stage (1, 2, 4, 8 or 16). Every other length (shorter and longer) is just a thin line until you focus it from the Song section of this menu. Tap a section to choose Pad or Mic. A pad sample drops in aligned to the start of its section — its red start segment sits on the section’s start edge, and section 1 starts on the red bar at the top, so it’s already quantized. Mic counts you in, records, then loops. Press and hold a filled section to select it, then drag inside the section to roll the waveform — the red start segment moves with your drag and snaps to the section’s start edge when you get close. Drag onto another section instead and letting go offers Move / Copy for that sample. To shift a sample to a different level, tap its section and choose Move / copy to the shorter or longer loop — or, while holding it, pull inwards for the loop half as long or push outwards for the loop twice as long and let go. Moving up keeps the sample’s real length and lands it at the same beat in the longer loop; moving down splits or fits it to the shorter cycle. Either way the view switches to the level it landed in, and that level keeps it when you focus away and back with the shorter / longer controls. The song starts paused — press the big play button under the stage when you’re ready.

About this mockup: it runs entirely in your browser. There’s no microphone yet — tapping a ring simulates a Koala-style take and synthesises a sound for that ring (hi-hat on the 1-beat, kick on the 2, groove on the 4, bass on the 8, chords beyond) — or use the demo-sound picker to put a kick, snare, tap, bass or chords on any ring you like — so you can feel the interaction model: ring = loop length, drag = roll, drag across rings = Tile / Move / Copy, chop = slice and roll each piece on its own. A production version would swap the simulated takes for real mic input and an audio engine underneath the same interface.
